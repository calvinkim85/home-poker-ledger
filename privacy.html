Home Poker Ledger is a calculator that runs entirely in your browser. It has no accounts, no server, and no database. The numbers you type are never sent anywhere. This page explains that in detail, and is honest about the two third parties that are involved despite it.

The short version

  • Everything you enter — player names, buy-ins, cash-outs, your currency and theme — is stored only in your own browser, and only so the page still has your game when you come back to it.
  • None of it is transmitted to us or to anyone else. There is nowhere for it to go: this site is static files with no backend.
  • Two third parties do see that a visit happened, and your IP address along with it: our host and our advertising provider. They are named below.
  • You can erase everything this site has stored by pressing Clear all in the app, or by clearing site data in your browser.

What is stored on your device

The app writes a single entry to your browser's localStorage under the key poker.ledger.v1. It contains exactly this:

WhatWhyHow long
Player names, as you typed themSo the seats still have names when you reopen the pageUntil you clear it
Buy-in amounts and cash-out amountsSo a game in progress survives a refresh or a closed tabUntil you clear it
Selected currency and standard buy-inSo you don't reset them every sessionUntil you clear it
Light or dark preferenceSo the page opens on the side you choseUntil you clear it

This never leaves your device. It is not synced, not backed up by us, and not readable by us. A game entered on your phone will not appear on your laptop — that is a direct consequence of this design, not an oversight.

Player names are the one field where you could enter someone else's personal information. You do not have to use real names, and nothing in the app requires them. Initials or nicknames work exactly as well.

Third parties that see your visit

We do not send them anything about your game. They see the request for the page itself, which necessarily includes your IP address.

Cloudflare Pages — hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare Pages, operated by Cloudflare, Inc. in the United States. Cloudflare receives your IP address and standard request information in order to deliver the page, and keeps its own server logs. We have no access to those logs and cannot query them. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Typefaces — served by us

The three typefaces this site uses are served from this site itself. They used to be loaded from Google Fonts, which meant Google received your IP address on every visit. That request no longer happens and Google Fonts is no longer involved in any way.

This site is supported by advertising supplied by Google AdSense, operated by Google LLC in the United States. If you are not seeing any ads on this site yet, the account is still pending approval and nothing described in this section is happening.

Where ads are shown, Google and its partners use cookies and similar device identifiers to serve and measure them, and may use them to personalise ads based on your prior visits to this and other sites. Google's use of advertising cookies is described in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

You can control this directly:

What we do not do

  • No analytics. There is no Google Analytics, no Plausible, no Fathom, no page-view counter of any kind.
  • No accounts, no email collection, no newsletter, no contact form.
  • No error or crash reporting service.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information. We have none to sell.
  • No AI or machine-learning processing of anything you enter.

Children

This site is not directed at children. It is a tool for settling accounts after a home card game, and it is not intended for, and not knowingly used to collect any information from, anyone under 14 in Korea or under 13 in the United States. Because the site collects nothing, there is nothing for us to delete; if you believe a child has entered personal information into their own browser on this site, pressing Clear all removes it.

How deletion actually works

There is no separate archive, no "deleted items" store, and no staging area that data passes through on its way out. It is held in exactly one place — your browser — so your deleting it is the deletion process. No approval from us is involved, and none is possible.

Two ways to do it, both immediate and both irreversible:

  • Press Clear all in the app.
  • Clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Either wipes the localStorage entry. Nothing is recoverable afterwards, by you or by us, because no copy exists anywhere else. Nothing is ever printed to paper.

What we never collect

We collect none of the categories Korean law treats as sensitive information — beliefs, political opinion, union or party membership, health, sex life, genetic or biometric data, criminal record, race or ethnicity — and none of the unique identifiers either: resident registration numbers, passport, driving licence, or foreign registration numbers. We also do not carry out pseudonymised-data processing.
